# Account identification and roster

**Status:** Available in the website Accounts area  
**Primary users:** ABM, sales, demand generation, and customer-marketing teams

## Purpose

The Accounts area organizes company-level visitors and known contacts around the website experience. It gives teams a roster for deciding which accounts matter and which personalized work should be created for them.

## Capabilities

- Browse an account roster associated with an Episode website.
- Search, sort, and filter the roster.
- Open an account detail panel without abandoning the larger account view.
- View account members or contacts when the available data supports them.
- Group work through campaign sets and segments.
- Select multiple accounts and run supported bulk actions.
- Inspect an individual member through a dedicated route.
- Use legacy account sub-routes through the consolidated overview where the application redirects them.

## How it works

The user opens **Accounts** for a specific website. The roster combines the account set, segment definitions, and member/contact data available to that site. Filters narrow the working group; opening a row reveals more context, while selection enables actions across several accounts.

The consolidated overview is the primary interface. Older account tabs such as matrix, build, domain, and settings are represented by focused parts of the newer workflow rather than requiring users to navigate separate operational silos.

## Important behavior

Account identification is probabilistic or source-dependent unless a visitor explicitly identifies themselves. Teams should not treat every company attribution as a verified person. Contact and activity data can also be incomplete because of privacy controls, source coverage, and anonymous traffic.

The roster is scoped to the selected website. An account’s presence there does not automatically create a personalized page or publish anything.
