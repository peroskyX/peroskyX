# Personalized account pages

**Status:** Available in the Accounts workflow  
**Primary users:** ABM, sales, and customer-marketing teams

## Purpose

Personalized account pages adapt an Episode experience for a selected company or segment. The workflow combines context, generation, preview, privacy choices, URL control, and publishing so personalization is deliberate rather than an invisible rule.

## Capabilities

- Generate page variants for selected accounts.
- Use an account or segment as the source context.
- Edit and preview account-specific copy before publishing.
- Choose supported generation and publish options.
- Set or edit a readable account-page slug.
- Choose a publication target.
- Apply the available privacy ladder or visibility setting.
- Detect and respond to source-context drift.
- Copy or export supported contact/page links for outreach.
- Generate pages for several selected roster entries through bulk controls.

## How it works

1. The team selects one or more accounts from the website roster.
2. Episode combines the base page with approved account context and generates the proposed experience.
3. The creator previews and edits the message, URL slug, privacy, and destination choices.
4. Publication makes the account page available at its selected target.
5. The team distributes the resulting link and observes engagement; later context changes can trigger a drift warning.

## Privacy and quality safeguards

Personalization must be recognizable as useful relevance, not surveillance. The team should avoid including inferred or sensitive details that a visitor would not expect to see. The privacy setting and link distribution strategy should match the content’s sensitivity.

Generated pages require human review for factual accuracy, tone, and collision-free slugs. Bulk generation accelerates production but does not eliminate the need to sample and validate outputs before wide outreach.
