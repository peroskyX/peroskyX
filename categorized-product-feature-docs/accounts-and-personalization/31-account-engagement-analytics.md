# Account engagement analytics

**Status:** Available within website analytics when account data is present  
**Primary users:** ABM, sales, and demand-generation teams

## Purpose

Account engagement analytics connects website activity to target-company operations. It helps teams understand which accounts and segments visited, what they read, and how engagement moved toward conversion.

## Capabilities

- Summarize named, visited, and converted people or account participants where identity data supports it.
- Compare segment performance.
- Inspect account stages and movement.
- Answer “who read what” through account/member activity views.
- Search, sort, and filter account analytics.
- Switch between account-oriented and segment-oriented perspectives.
- Focus broader analytics on a selected account.
- Connect engagement evidence to personalized page and roster workflows.

## How it works

The user opens analytics for an Episode website and chooses the account-focused view or filter. Account and segment summaries narrow aggregate traffic into the target-company context. Detail views expose the supported content interactions and stage information, helping the team decide where follow-up is justified.

The same data can be read from two useful directions: account view answers what one organization did, while segment view compares the performance of a planned audience group.

## Interpretation and privacy

Identity quality varies. Anonymous or partially identified activity should not be presented as a confirmed named person. Ad blockers, consent choices, network attribution, and source coverage can all produce incomplete evidence.

Engagement indicates attention, not buying intent by itself. Teams should combine it with known relationship context and use proportionate outreach. Access to person- and company-level activity should follow the organization’s privacy and data-retention rules.
