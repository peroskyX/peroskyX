# Billing, plans, credits, and usage

**Status:** Available to the account owner  
**Primary users:** Account owners and budget holders

## Purpose

Billing settings explain the account’s plan, AI-credit balance, and website usage, then provide the actions needed to change or maintain the subscription. Episode separates AI usage from included hosting and lead collection.

## Capabilities

- View the current plan and subscription status.
- Review credits used against the account’s monthly grant.
- See relevant website usage.
- Buy additional credits through the purchase flow.
- Upgrade or otherwise change plan through the available subscription actions.
- Enter a guarded downgrade workflow that explains the target plan and affected websites.
- Pause or manage billing through the billing portal where offered.
- Reactivate a subscription scheduled for cancellation or otherwise eligible for reactivation.
- Receive referral information and credit rewards when the server makes a referral offer available.
- Show billing status warnings or notices requiring attention.

## How it works

The account owner opens **Billing & usage**. Plan, status, and credit information are loaded for the account. Subscription actions open the relevant purchase, portal, downgrade, or reactivation experience. Downgrades require confirmation because limits and website eligibility can change at the next plan state.

Credits are pooled at account level; all members draw from the same monthly grant. The Members view can show that shared meter in an access-management context.

## Important behavior

Only the account owner can see billing. Hosting, page publication, and lead collection do not consume AI credits; agent generation and other AI-assisted work can. “Unlimited pages” does not imply unlimited active Episodes, credits, seats, analytics features, or custom domains.

Plan changes can affect access to analytics, experiments, workspaces, seats, custom domains, and badge removal. The downgrade flow should be read carefully before confirmation.
