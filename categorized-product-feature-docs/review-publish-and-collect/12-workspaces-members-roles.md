# Workspaces, members, and roles

**Status:** Available; workspaces and additional seats are paid capabilities  
**Primary users:** Account owners, administrators, agencies, and multi-brand teams

## Purpose

Accounts provide the billing and membership boundary, while workspaces separate clients, brands, or operational groups. Roles and workspace access determine who can see and manage shared work.

## Capabilities

- Invite people to an account and assign their access.
- Use owner, admin, and editor responsibilities represented by the product.
- Separate work into multiple workspaces.
- Control which workspaces an individual member can reach.
- View current members and outstanding invitations.
- Review status, role, workspace access, and per-member AI usage where exposed.
- Approve join requests when domain-based joining is enabled.
- Allow eligible company email domains to request access.
- Handle account ownership offers.
- View the shared monthly account-credit pool.

## How it works

The account owner or an authorized administrator opens **Members**, sends an invitation, and chooses the recipient’s role and workspace reach. Invitees accept through the invitation flow. If allowed-domain joining is enabled, people using the configured non-personal domain can request to join and an authorized user approves or rejects those requests.

All members draw from the account’s shared AI-credit allocation rather than receiving independent seat balances. The members view can show usage context alongside access management.

## Permissions and boundaries

Only the owner can see and manage billing. Member viewing, invitations, join approvals, and join-policy changes are individually permission-gated; editors do not receive administrator controls. Billing remains at account scope even when work is divided into workspaces.

Inviting a member does not automatically give them access to every workspace. Conversely, removing workspace access should be distinguished from removing the person from the account.
