# Search results, AI Answers, and reports

**Status:** Available when the required search data is connected and collected  
**Primary users:** Organic-growth leaders measuring outcomes and visibility

## Purpose

These Grow Search views separate detailed outcomes from day-to-day planning. Results describe achieved search impact, AI Answers focuses on visibility in supported answer experiences, and monthly reports provide a period-based summary.

## Capabilities

- Browse a dedicated Search results view.
- Search and filter the results using URL-represented state.
- Inspect AI-answer visibility in a dedicated view.
- Search and filter AI Answers without losing the selected state on navigation.
- Open a report for a specific month.
- Connect reported outcomes back to plan and overview context.
- Handle empty, loading, missing-connection, and unavailable-data states.

## How it works

The user begins with the desired reporting surface. In Results or AI Answers they apply search terms and filters to narrow the entries under review. Because key selections are represented in the URL, a filtered view can be revisited or shared with another authorized teammate.

Monthly reports are addressed by month and consolidate the available period context. They complement, rather than replace, live overview and detailed-result exploration.

## Interpretation and limits

Search results and AI mentions rely on connected sources, supported monitoring, and data availability. A missing entry is not definitive proof that the organization has no visibility anywhere. Similarly, correlation between Episode work and a later result does not by itself establish causation.

Month-based reporting should be read with the correct time zone and comparison period in mind. Recently connected accounts may have incomplete historical coverage, and search systems can report with delay.
