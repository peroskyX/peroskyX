# Article workflow, quality, and SEO

**Status:** Available  
**Primary users:** Writers, editors, approvers, and organic-growth teams

## Purpose

Episode surrounds article drafting with the checks and decisions needed to turn a draft into publishable content. This includes evidence, quality signals, search presentation, approval state, scheduling, and publishing blockers.

## Capabilities

- Request or incorporate evidence and research in the writing process.
- Score or assess content quality through the editor’s quality workflow.
- Configure SEO title, description, URL, and related metadata.
- Preview search and social presentation.
- Inherit relevant site-level SEO defaults where configured.
- Send a draft for approval, approve it, or send it back for changes.
- Detect blockers that prevent publishing.
- Schedule publication or publish when requirements are satisfied.
- Use an article-specific agent to propose revisions.
- Preserve and restore unsaved draft work when supported by the editor state.

## How it works

The writer develops content and metadata in the article editor. Quality and evidence tools identify gaps that deserve attention; they do not replace editorial review. The editor previews how the post will appear to readers and in distribution surfaces.

When the draft is ready, it enters the approval path. An approver can accept it or return it with the expectation of further work. Scheduling and publication become available only when required content, permissions, and destination configuration are valid.

## Important behavior

Quality scores are decision support, not an objective guarantee of accuracy or ranking. Sources and claims still require human verification. Search and social previews approximate external presentation; search engines and networks can rewrite or cache metadata.

Publishing blockers should be resolved rather than bypassed because they commonly represent missing destination fields, permissions, or required metadata. Approval describes editorial readiness and is distinct from the external CMS successfully accepting the post.
