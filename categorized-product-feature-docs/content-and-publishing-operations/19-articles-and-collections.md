# Articles and collections

**Status:** Available  
**Primary users:** Content marketers, editors, and SEO teams

## Purpose

The Articles area is Episode’s structured content workspace. Collections organize related entries, while each article has a dedicated editor, preview, publication context, and agent conversation.

## Capabilities

- Browse article collections and their entries.
- Search the article index and move into a specific collection.
- Open an article in a structured editor.
- Switch between editing and previewing the content.
- Compose content from supported structured blocks.
- Select, crop, focus, and describe a cover image.
- Manage article title, summary, author, tags, URL, and other post details.
- Associate an article with a Grow plan item or research context.
- Track signup or conversion attribution associated with content where available.
- Keep a post-specific agent thread for assisted revisions.

## How it works

1. A user opens the Articles area and selects a collection.
2. They create or choose an entry, then edit its structured content and metadata.
3. Preview shows how the article will read without leaving the workflow.
4. The post agent can help revise or develop the article using its current context.
5. The user completes workflow and destination requirements before scheduling or publishing.

## Important behavior

Collections are organizational and destination context can add separate publishing rules. An article can be drafted in Episode before its external CMS destination is fully configured.

The editor includes protection for unsaved work, including backup/restore behavior represented in the article workflow. Users should still respond to unsaved-change prompts before navigating away. Cover-image crop, focal point, and alternative text serve different purposes and should each be reviewed.
